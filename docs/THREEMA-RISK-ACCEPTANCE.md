# Private-use risk acceptance

This project is intended for private, single-user, non-commercial use of one Threema identity.

- Self-use is not an explicit exemption from the service terms.
- The bridge may be blocked; its linked device or authorization may be revoked.
- No anti-detection, rate-limit bypass, device-limit bypass, or dependency on the bridge for account
  recovery is permitted.
- Distribution, hosting for others, or commercial use is outside this intended scope.

This records intended use, not approval from Threema. Dedicated test identities should stay
isolated from daily accounts. The phone remains the account authority and recovery path.
